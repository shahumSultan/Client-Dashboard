# Landing (/) surface brief

Scope: the portal's public landing page, frontend/app/page.tsx + components/landing/LandingPage.tsx. Mode: Persuade.
Audience: invited clients arriving from an invitation email or the website; returning clients and the Enigma-Cube team signing in. Action: sign in (or open the portal when already signed in). Proof: the portal's real contents (timeline, comments, deliverables, invoices); no testimonials or metrics.
Constraints: world is enigma-cube.com (PRODUCT.md Brand Commitments). Invite-only: no self-serve sales pitch.
